What this does

Tell the program how pixels in the video match real metres on the road. Click spots on the ground that you also measured with a tape. Nothing is guessed: the numbers you type are the numbers used.

A. A rectangle on the road

Pick a rectangle you know the sides of (for example a zebra block). Press Start, then click its 4 corners in order, going around.

B. Check with a tape distance (needed)

Click two ground spots, then type the tape distance between them. This is never used to fit the mapping; it tests it.

C. Area where the mapping is valid

Click the corners of the road area around your spots, then press Finish area.

Points

Change a metre value or role here if a click was off. Role "check" points are never used for the fit.

Result

Add 4 corner points, one check and the valid area.
Counting lines are drawn across the road. Click the start and the end of a line, then name it. The arrow shows which way counted vehicles move.

Lines

For each approach, draw the area where queue and delay are measured and click its stop point. The stop point's metres come from step 1, so save the ground points first.

Approach areas

Road width across each approach at the stop line. Click one side of the road, then the other side, along the stop line. The width is read through the ground mapping, so it is a derived value (label it so in the report). A tape width is better when you have one.
